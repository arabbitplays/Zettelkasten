## Layered Materials

- most real world materials are neither homogenous nor just one kind of material at a single point, but multiple layers of different material
	- for example a metal with some coating and blood or dirt on top of the coating
- this can either be raytraced explicitly or approximated by material combination operators, combining multiple base materials into a layered version
- to make all this look good, very high fidelity textures and a lot of rendering time is needed -> more of an offline technique
